## Documentation style — mandatory owner rule

Avoid self-directed instructions, assistant self-reference and signs of an AI assistant in product documentation. Write factual descriptions and reader-facing instructions. Do not add phrases such as “I changed”, “as an AI”, “the assistant prepared” or “the owner uploads manually” to README files, changelogs or release notes. Keep internal agent workflow and handoff directions in AI instructions, not product docs. Describing actual AI features and the builder’s LLM review prompt remains appropriate.
